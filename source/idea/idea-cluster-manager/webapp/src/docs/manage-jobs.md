## Manage jobs

All users: Active and Completed show jobs across users. Queues and Licenses retain their create and edit workflows. Nodes lists compute nodes. Use My jobs for your own submissions.

Active jobs show scheduler status reasons. Completed jobs keep their final status and reason. In **Queues**, a blocked queue shows its limit and how much is in use.

Nodes left **down** or **unknown** beyond the configured unavailable-node timeout are reclaimed automatically. Nodes marked keep forever are exempt.
