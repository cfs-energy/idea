## Settings

Open **Administration > Settings**, then select **Desktops** or search a configuration key. Each group has its own subpage; a search match scrolls to its editor. The single daily schedule table and desktop defaults retain their guided editors; additional lifecycle settings have typed editors.

Choose **Edit** for one section, then **Save** or **Cancel**; a card shows at most one badge, either **Applies after restart** or **Applies on next upgrade**. **Advanced** is collapsed at the bottom and opens automatically for a search match.

See Settings help for the full group list and the save behaviour of guided editors.
