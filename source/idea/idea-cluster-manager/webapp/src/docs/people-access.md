## People and access

Users and Groups manage accounts and memberships. Desktop permissions manages capability profiles. Reconciliation runs provides on-demand actions and reports; edit periodic policy under **Settings > Users and sign-in > Account synchronization**.

Both views load saved settings from the settings table. In Settings, **Scheduled reconciliation** reflects the saved on/off value and saves immediately. Advanced edits apply only after **Save reconciliation settings**; saves notify the periodic worker. **Scheduled runs** shows Off, Due now, or the last periodic checkpoint plus the saved interval.

In Reconciliation runs, **Last run at / result** shows the latest manual or periodic result and whether it was a dry run or apply. Status refreshes every ten seconds. Choose **Preview changes** first, then **Run reconciliation** after reviewing the report. These actions use saved settings and are available even when scheduled reconciliation is off. Reopening the page restores the saved report; a notice identifies reports limited to the first 100 rows.
