## My jobs

Active and Completed show only your jobs, including when you are an administrator. Use Submit to choose a submission form and saved template, or Write script to prepare a script.

Active jobs show **Status Reason** for capacity waits, blocking queue usage, provisioning errors, and exhausted retries. A capacity wait is not a permanent rejection and has no queue-position or start-time estimate.

Completed jobs retain **Ran**, **Failed**, **Held**, or **Deleted**, including owner cancellations and system deletions. Details preserve the reason. Cost details show recorded savings and budget impact when available; missing pricing reads **Price not available**.
