# Scratch cleanup

When a job finishes, its numbered scratch folder gets a removal date: 30 days later for a success and 60 for anything else. Named folders are never touched. Every check runs again on that date. Off until you turn it on in settings.

A folder goes only if the job is over, its owner has no active job, nothing active points at it, everything in it dates from that job's run, and the owner has had two weekly notices.

Backfill is for folders from before cleanup was on. Previewing removes nothing.

Stop all removals takes effect within a couple of seconds.
