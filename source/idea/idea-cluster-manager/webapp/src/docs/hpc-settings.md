## Settings

Open **Administration > Settings**, then select **Job limits and placement** or search a configuration key. Each group has its own subpage; a search match scrolls to its editor. Admission, placement and scheduler tuning are editable. Queue-specific overrides remain in Manage jobs.

Choose **Edit** for one section, then **Save** or **Cancel**; badges appear only for settings labeled **Restart required** or **Applies on next upgrade**. **Advanced** is collapsed at the bottom and opens automatically for a search match.

See Settings help for the full group list and the save behaviour of guided editors.
